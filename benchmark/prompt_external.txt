You are evaluating physical statements under a typed grammar called TIE 2.

Labels:
- valid: the statement is admissible under the rules.
- invalid: forbidden sum, product, root, or unit.
- process: a process or relation, not a unit.
- candidate: an admissible combination without a standard name or measurement protocol; it is NOT a prediction.

Rules:
- The L exponent must be in -3..3. The T, R, V, Wb, Gr exponents must be in -1, 0, 1.
- Reject T^2, T^-2, L^4, L^-4, R^2, R^-2, V^2, Wb^2, Gr^2, V^-2, Wb^-2, Gr^-2, and higher.
- s^2, s^3, and sqrt(s^2) are inadmissible.
- Sums require the same dimension, tensor type, and quantity class.
- Torque vs energy, pressure vs energy density, and angular velocity vs frequency are invalid sums.
- Products cannot exceed the exponent budget.
- Roots of m^2 or m^3 are allowed; roots of primitives or of s^2 are forbidden.

Input format: each line is `ID | statement`.

Output format: a JSON array only, with one object per ID:

[{"id":"B-001","label":"invalid"}]

Do not omit any ID. Do not explain. Do not use code fences.

Example:
Input:
B-001 | The unit `T²` is admissible.
Output:
[{"id":"B-001","label":"invalid"}]
